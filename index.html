What we do

Everyday jobs and awkward ones. Not sure what's wrong? Ring and describe it, and Ryan will give you straight advice.

  • Servicing

    Services for all makes and models, with dealer-trained know-how for VW Group, Kia, Hyundai and Vauxhall.

  • MOTs

    Book your MOT, often alongside a service. Customers tell us the price is spot on.

  • Repairs and diagnostics

    Warning lights, strange noises, brakes, clutches, exhausts and suspension. Tracked down and fixed.

  • Air conditioning

    Air con servicing and regassing, so it blows cold again when you need it.

  • Turbos

    Turbo supply and fitting.

  • Wheel alignment

    Four-wheel alignment on our Supertracker laser aligner, to stop uneven tyre wear and pulling.

  • Welding

    Specialist welding and repairs, done properly, even when it means working late.

  • Recovery

    Broken down nearby? Ring 01274 830310 and ask about recovery to the workshop.

  • Vans and commercials

    Vans and commercial vehicles as well as cars, as it says on our sign. Keeping your van on the road keeps you earning.

Dealer-trained staff for VW Group, Kia, Hyundai and Vauxhall. Every other make and model is welcome too.
